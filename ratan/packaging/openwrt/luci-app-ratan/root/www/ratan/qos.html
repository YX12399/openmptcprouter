What you're looking at. Per-WAN bandwidth shaping. Each WAN has three traffic classes: realtime (VideoCall, VoIP) gets a guaranteed floor regardless of what the others are doing; bulk takes whatever's left; background is capped so it can't push realtime out of its floor. During a WAN drop, the other WAN's tree still honors realtime's floor — that's the "Teams survives the drop while iperf3 absorbs the hit" property. Bars below show current usage; the small notch is the configured floor.
